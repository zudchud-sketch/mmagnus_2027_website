How to register

  1. Open the registration page.
  2. Enter your name and valid contact details.
  3. Select your department and preferred event.
  4. For team events, coordinate with 2–4 other members.
  5. Submit the form and follow the fest desk’s confirmation instructions.

Before you participate

  • Carry your college ID card.
  • Arrive at the venue 20 minutes early.
  • Follow event rules and volunteer instructions.
  • Keep your registration confirmation handy.
